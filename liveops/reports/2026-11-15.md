## Proposed changes

Three changes. None of them is applied in this pull request. Sample size and the missing fortnight split are too small to support a player-facing experiment.

### 1. Hold every player-facing setting

Leave Remote Config, prices, ad placements, and difficulty at the values live on 15 November 2026 through the next fortnight.

- Expected movement: day-1 stays at 0 / 1 on 2.5.0 and 1 / 5 across versions; day-7 stays at 0 / 1; ad revenue stays near $0.033 per 28 days; purchase revenue stays at $0; plays per player stays near 9.333 on 2.5.0 and 14.231 across versions. This hold claims no lift.
- Holdout: 100% of players remain on the 15 November 2026 production configuration. There is no treatment arm.
- Rollback rule: if a player-facing setting changes anyway, restore the 15 November 2026 value within 24 hours when the next scoreboard shows day-1 at 0 retained among at least 5 eligible installs, or ad revenue at $0 while `game_started` is at least 56 in that window.

### 2. Publish the fortnight split and a daily-user count

Add a last-14-day block and a prior-14-day block to the scoreboard, plus distinct daily active users (a player with `session_started`, `app_launched`, or `game_started` on that UTC day). Keep the current 28-day blocks in the same payload.

- Expected movement: no movement in day-1, day-7, revenue, or plays. The next report can compute ad revenue per daily user, purchase revenue per daily user, and plays per daily user for each fortnight.
- Holdout: no player holdout. This is a read-model change. Before relying on it, the sum of the two fortnights must equal the current 28-day events, installs, sessions, game starts, ad revenue, and purchase revenue.
- Rollback rule: if those sums differ by any event, or by more than $0.0001 of revenue, or if `trusted` is false, drop the new blocks and serve the 28-day payload recorded in this report.

### 3. Count rewarded-ad participation without a new placement

Publish rewarded completions and distinct rewarded participants. Daily participation is distinct players with a rewarded completion that UTC day, divided by distinct daily active users that day. Add no placement, cap change, or price change.

- Expected movement: participation becomes measurable. Counting alone should leave day-1, day-7, revenue per daily user, and plays per daily user where they are. There is no participation baseline in this payload, so no participation lift is claimed.
- Holdout: count every client that already sends the event. No player sees a new rewarded prompt under this change.
- Rollback rule: if rewarded completions in a window exceed `session_started` in that same window, drop the participation field. Keep `interstitial_shown`, `ad_revenue_usd`, and `iap_revenue_usd` as the ad and purchase fields.
