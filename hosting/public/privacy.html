Flappy Jet Privacy Policy

Last updated November 6, 2026.

Flappy Jet stores game progress on your device and sends gameplay, purchase, and crash events to Firebase and to our game server so we can keep the game running and understand what to improve.

Advertising uses Google AdMob and Unity Ads. On iOS we ask before using the advertising identifier. In regions that require it, a consent form appears before ads load. Rewarded ads stay available if you buy No Ads. No Ads removes interstitial ads.

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the App Store. We keep a purchase token so a reward is granted once.

We do not sell personal information. To ask for deletion of data tied to your device id, email flappyjet2025@gmail.com.
